# Nova Ascendancy

## 🪐 Overview
Nova Ascendancy is a turn-based strategy game where players assume the role of intergalactic rulers. Starting with two planets, players must expand their empire through warfare, diplomacy, and resource management over 365 turns to achieve galactic domination.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Develop an immersive and competitive turn-based strategy experience.
- Implement mechanics for combat, diplomacy, and planetary upgrades.
- Design an intuitive UI for seamless player interactions.
- Develop AI-driven opponents with adaptive decision-making.
- Ensure balanced gameplay for long-term engagement.

## 🚀 Features
- **Turn-Based Strategy:** Players act sequentially to expand their empire.
- **Four Playable Factions:** Each with unique traits and starting conditions.
- **Tactical Combat System:** Turn-based space battles with strategic formations.
- **Planet Management:** Upgrade planets for economic and defensive advantages.
- **Varied Ship Types:** Attack-heavy, defensive, and balanced options.
- **Diplomacy & Trade:** Form alliances, trade resources, or declare war.
- **Resource Economy:** Manage finances, military upkeep, and morale.
- **AI Opponents:** Adaptive AI ensures a challenging experience.
